MTN MoMo vs M-Pesa vs Airtel Money: Which is Best for Your Country?

MTN MoMo, M-Pesa, and Airtel Money are the three biggest mobile money networks in East Africa. Workatap supports all three across Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda and Zambia.

MTN MoMo is the top network in Uganda, Rwanda and Zambia with deep rural coverage. M-Pesa dominates Kenya and Tanzania with over 30 million users and is the most trusted mobile money brand in East Africa.

Airtel Money is available across all 5 supported countries and regularly runs zero-fee promotions. Tigo Pesa is a strong option for Tanzania users. Both make great backup networks for Workatap cashouts.

Our recommendation: use your country's primary network (MTN or M-Pesa) and keep Airtel as a backup. Having two mobile money accounts means you're never stuck.

FINANCIAL TIPS

5 Smart Ways to Save Money as a Young East African in 2026

Managing money well is one of the most important skills any young Ugandan can learn. Whether you earn in Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda or Zambia — from a job, business, or platforms like Workatap — how you manage what you earn determines your financial future.

1. Pay yourself first. Before spending any money you receive, set aside at least 10% into savings. Even a small daily saving adds up — UGX 5,000 in Uganda or KES 170 in Kenya becomes a meaningful amount by month end.

2. Use mobile money savings. MTN MoMo, M-Pesa, and Airtel Money all offer savings wallet features. Use them to keep your Workatap earnings separate from your spending money.

3. Avoid unnecessary airtime spending. Data bundles are almost always cheaper than paying per MB. Buy weekly or monthly bundles to save more.

4. Track your earnings and spending. Keep a simple notebook or phone note of what comes in and what goes out each week.

5. Grow your income streams. Do not rely on one source. Combine your regular income with Workatap earnings to build multiple streams of income.
