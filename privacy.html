Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Workatap ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use Workatap, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name and username
  • Your phone number (used for account registration and mobile money payouts)
  • Your email address (optional, used for account recovery)
  • Your device type and browser information (for app functionality)
  • Activity data such as games played, tasks completed, and referrals made

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Workatap account
  • Process your earnings and mobile money withdrawals (MTN MoMo / Airtel Money)
  • Send you important account notifications
  • Improve our platform and user experience
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which is protected by industry-standard security measures. We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following situations:

  • With mobile money providers (MTN, Airtel) solely to process your withdrawal requests
  • When required by law or a valid legal request

5. Cookies & Advertising

Workatap uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Workatap is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support.workatap@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of Workatap after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
